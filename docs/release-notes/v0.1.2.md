## New
- **Linux and macOS builds**: besides the Windows installer, every release now ships a `.deb` and an `.AppImage` for Linux and a `.dmg` for macOS (Apple silicon).
  - They are new and less tested than Windows.
  - System audio, click highlights, window snapping, OCR, e-mail attachments and "Open with" are Windows-only for now.
- **Copy text (OCR)**: recognises the text in a screenshot and copies it to the clipboard. It uses the OCR engine built into Windows and is available in the editor and the gallery menu.
- **Recording**:
  - **Pause and resume**: the parts are joined into a single video when you stop.
  - **Webcam overlay**: a round camera bubble you can drag and resize with the mouse wheel. It is recorded with the screen.
- **Video editor**: "Edit" on a recording opens a player with a trim bar. Keep only the part you want, or export it as an **animated GIF**.
- **Gallery**:
  - **Drag images in**: dropping image files (PNG, JPG, BMP, GIF, WebP, TIFF) or MP4 videos on the gallery copies them into the captures folder. The originals stay untouched.
  - **Paste with Ctrl+V**: an image on the clipboard, or files copied in Explorer, become new captures.

## Changed
- The recording panel is wider, to fit the pause and webcam controls.
