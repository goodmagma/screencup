## New
- **Copy and paste shapes in the editor**: with shapes selected, Ctrl+C copies them instead of the whole image, and Ctrl+V pastes copies offset a little, as in PowerPoint. Each further paste lands a little further away, and the pasted copies are selected. Shapes can also be pasted into another editor window. With no shape selected, Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V still copy the image and paste a picture.
- **Move shapes with the arrow keys**: the arrow keys move the selected shapes by 1 pixel, and Shift+arrow by 10 pixels. Holding a key down counts as one step for undo.
