## New
- **Pixel-precise selection**: the magnifier next to the cursor now shows the cursor position in screen pixels (the selection size while dragging) and the colour under the cursor. The arrow keys move the cursor by one pixel, ten with Shift.
- **Color picker**:
  - Press **C** during a region capture to copy the colour under the cursor as `#RRGGBB`.
  - It is also a mode of its own, from the tray menu, the gallery's capture menu or a global hotkey (default **Ctrl+Alt+C**, configurable in Settings). A click copies the colour.
- **Shift in the editor**, as in graphics editors:
  - Rectangles, ellipses, blur, magnifier, callouts, braces and the crop area become squares and circles.
  - Lines, arrows, polygon edges and dragged line ends snap to 15° steps.
  - Pressing or releasing Shift with the mouse still updates the shape at once.
  - Shift on a resize handle turns the selected shape into a square or circle.
  - Alt still makes squares and circles, as before.
- **Keyboard shortcuts summary**: the **?** button in the gallery title bar, or **F1**, lists every shortcut: the global hotkeys as configured, and the keys of the capture overlay, the gallery, the editor and the video editor.

## Changed
- Dependencies updated (Vite React plugin, Lucide icons, screen capture library and other minor versions).
