## New
- **Select several shapes in the editor**: Ctrl+click adds a shape to the selection or takes it out, and Ctrl+A selects every shape. Each selected shape shows its own frame and handles, as in PowerPoint. The selection moves together when dragged, and color, size and Delete apply to all of it.
- **Undo a deletion in the gallery**: Ctrl+Z brings the last deleted captures back from the trash, up to 20 deletions back. The message shown after a deletion mentions Ctrl+Z.
